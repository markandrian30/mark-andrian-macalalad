About

Mark is a chemical engineer from the University of the Philippines Diliman. His research uses computers to find new medicines: screening thousands of natural compounds — from Philippine plants, algae, fungi and bacteria — against disease targets such as diabetes proteins, African swine fever virus, Nipah virus, dengue and Zika. He also builds AI systems, including Kibo, a full-duplex voice assistant on a Raspberry Pi.

Project: Kibo

ME3 - Full-Duplex Voice AI Agent

AI 231 · University of the Philippines Diliman

A voice assistant on a Raspberry Pi 5 that you talk to like a person in the room — no wake word, no push-to-talk. It can be interrupted mid-sentence and keeps long jobs running in the background.

  • Builds websites and apps, and publishes them to GitHub Pages
  • Writes and runs machine-learning jobs on the university's DGX servers
  • Answers chemistry questions from PubChem and RCSB
  • Checks flights and movie showtimes, plays music, manages the calendar
